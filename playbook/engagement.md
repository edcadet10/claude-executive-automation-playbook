# Run the engagement

**Deliverable:** The executive can produce a source-linked month-end draft, explain a discrepancy to the controller and recover a failed run. With approved access, use one client's closed period. Use the fictional dealership pack to rehearse the same decisions when access is pending.

## 30-minute fit call

| Minutes | Jeff asks or demonstrates | Evidence to leave with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 | “Take me to the last operating meeting. Which decision waited for a number?” | One decision, its owner and the consequence of being late |
| 5–12 | Executive shares the current workflow using redacted material. Where does Claude lose an entity, repeat a lookup or produce a number nobody trusts? | Sketch of sources, manual steps, elapsed time and a real exception |
| 12–20 | Jeff opens the TradeProof tool contract and an Agent Sarah teaching example. Executive names which part of each pattern transfers to the business. | Fit on integrations, technical depth and teaching style |
| 20–27 | Agree on one store, one closed period, one reviewer, and whether a sandbox or approved export is available. | Candidate pilot and fallback |
| 27–30 | Agree on 14 or 21 contact hours, delivery dates, owners, access lead times and whether discovery/prep are separately billed. | Written scope and date confirmation |

## Discovery and preparation

Schedule a separate 45–60 minute discovery call. Ask the executive to drive their current Claude workflow once. Time active work and elapsed waiting separately. Save the original request, a redacted answer, every correction, and the source that settled it. Record actual observations in a private copy of this table.

| Decision field | Dealership example | Fill in with the client |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decision and approver | Whether the GM should ask for a margin variance review. Controller approves the brief. | Decision, named owner and deputy |
| Entity boundary | One store, one CRM portal/pipeline, one accounting company, one approved policy library | Actual IDs, authorized identity, storage and retention owner |
| Reporting policy | Closed calendar month, USD, accrual, management-approved floor in force during that month | Fiscal calendar, timezone, basis, currency, cutoff, rounding and close sign-off |

Before day one, Jeff prepares an entity-specific context sheet, normalized extracts, a hand-calculated reference, an exception case and a clean learner environment. The controller supplies a signed-off example and the policy version. The administrator identifies sandbox and delegated access. If a dependency is late, record its owner and use the synthetic pack for that module.

## Acceptance and value measurement

Give the executive a blank session and the previous month's accepted report as a reference. Ask them to select the entity, rebuild the draft, show the three source records, explain percentage points and rerun the same period. Swap one source to another entity and observe what they do. A pass requires correct figures, explicit treatment of missing evidence, a blocked mismatch and one reviewable result per input revision. A numerical miss or an invented source reopens the exercise.

Repeat the baseline stopwatch on comparable periods and record preparation minutes, correction count, exceptions and review time. Example worksheet: `period | source completeness | manual minutes | assisted minutes | corrections | review minutes | approver`. Compute observed hours saved as the paired difference in total human minutes divided by 60. Investigate a slower run and include training or exception-handling time in its explanation. At 30 days, review three real cycles or agree on the next available close if fewer have occurred.
