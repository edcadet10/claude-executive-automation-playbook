Before we meet for the workshop

  1. Assess fit 30 minutes

    You show me a recent decision and where Claude fell short. I demonstrate a comparable tool from my TradeProof work. We leave with one candidate project and a decision on whether to proceed.

  2. Scope one pilot Separate 45–60 minute discovery meeting

    We replay your current workflow. I document the sources, baseline time, corrections, business entity and reviewer. You leave with a one-page scope, an acceptance test and a short access list.

  3. Prepare your case My preparation

    With an approved sample, I reconcile the expected result, prepare the Claude workspace and create one failure for us to diagnose. You receive the reference result and tailored agenda before day one.

Discovery and preparation are separate from the 14–21 workshop hours. We confirm dates, access and commercial scope on the fit call.

What we’ll build together

  1. Day one · Build a working pilot 7 hours

    You drive the existing process. We place the business rules and metric definitions in an entity-specific Claude project, reconcile one known result, and build a reusable Claude Code skill. You run it from a fresh session. You leave with a sample-data pilot and the evidence needed to check its output.

  2. Day two · Connect, challenge and operate it 7 hours

    We define the MCP reads, test the wrong entity or period, and give a reviewer agent the evidence behind the draft. For a dealership, we map HubSpot deals, the QuickBooks P&L and the policy in Microsoft 365. We use approved sandbox access or exports. You rehearse a failed run, replay it, and take over the review queue.

  3. Optional day three · Extend the pilot 7 hours

    Choose one outcome. We can commission another approved source and rehearse scheduling, alerts and restore on your private host. Or we can build an investment case around a community’s rent roll and show how an occupancy change affects net operating income.

What you leave with

You receive the workspace, Claude Code skill, reviewer-agent definition, connector map and operating runbook. We record who owns each remaining access or deployment step.

The acceptance test is yours to run. Start in a fresh session. Reproduce the result, trace its figures, catch a bad input, and replay without duplicating the draft. We compare time and corrections with the baseline and choose the first real cycle to review.

Example pilot · A dealership month-end brief

These results come from three recorded calls to the lab’s read-only MCP server. The server read fictional records. Open the calls below or reproduce the session in your own checkout.

Ask for North Store’s September figures

get_reporting_packet("north-store", "2026-09") returned $250,000 in income and $207,500 in cost of goods sold. Gross profit was $42,500. At the policy floor, it would have been $45,000.

The figures trace to QBO-PL-09, POL-MARGIN-04 and CRM-SNAPSHOT-09. Inspect the source records.

Ask for another business or reporting period

The same server rejected South Store for September and North Store for August. Both requests returned an error before releasing a financial packet. Ask the executive to predict the outcome, then inspect the returned values. Open all three recorded calls.

Review a plausible explanation for the miss

An example draft says discounting caused the decline. The packet contains a policy comparison, one month of financial totals and open deals. It contains no pricing bridge or prior-month results. The reviewer should return the sentence for revision and ask the controller for the breakdown.
